About Sadana Green Society

An environmental and community-focused organisation working with farmers across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka.

Who we are

Sadana Green Society (SGS) is an environmental and community-focused organisation working to promote climate change mitigation, agroforestry development, sustainable agriculture, farmer livelihood enhancement and carbon-related programmes.

Our mission is to support farming communities through sustainable land-use practices, tree plantation initiatives, environmental awareness and collaborative development projects.

We work with farmers, field teams, technical partners and institutions across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka to encourage responsible environmental practices and strengthen rural livelihoods.

Our mission

To contribute to a greener and more sustainable future by empowering farmers and supporting environmental protection.

Our vision

Thriving farming communities, healthier ecosystems and a climate-resilient future for generations to come.

Our projects

Eight areas of work that connect farmers, land and climate.

  • Agroforestry and new plantation projectsPlanting suitable trees on farmland to improve land use, tree cover and long-term income.Why agroforestry
  • Existing plantation projectsSupporting farmers to look after and monitor plantations already in the ground.
  • Carbon credit programmesHelping farmers join carbon programmes and share in the benefits.Benefits of carbon credits
  • Sustainable agriculturePractical, resource-efficient farming that makes farms more resilient.
  • Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)A water-saving paddy method that also cuts methane emissions.How AWD works
  • Farmer livelihood enhancementTraining, guidance and programme access that help farm families earn more securely.
  • Climate change mitigationReducing greenhouse gas emissions and storing carbon in trees and soil.Environment
  • Environmental awarenessHelping communities understand why trees, soil and water matter and how to protect them.

How we work with farmers

A simple, accountable process from first conversation to results.

  1. ListenWe meet farming communities, understand local needs and explain each programme openly.
  2. EnrolInterested farmers are registered and their land and details are recorded.
  3. ImplementField teams support farmers with practices on the ground.
  4. Monitor and reportWe follow up, keep clear records and share progress with partners.
